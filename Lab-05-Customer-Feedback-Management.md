
# Lab 05 – Customer Feedback & Satisfaction Tracking

## Overview
This lab focused on documenting and managing customer feedback using Bitrix24 CRM.

The exercise simulated a customer providing feedback about a previous support experience. The customer was satisfied with the assistance received but identified delayed communication as an area for improvement.

The lab demonstrated how to record feedback, review customer concerns, create follow-up activities, and document service improvement actions.

## Objectives
- Create and organize customer feedback records.
- Associate feedback with existing customer contacts.
- Document customer satisfaction and areas of dissatisfaction.
- Review customer feedback internally.
- Create follow-up activities.
- Identify opportunities for service improvement.
- Maintain a record of customer feedback and actions.

## Environment
- CRM Platform: Bitrix24
- Application Type: Browser-based CRM
- CRM Modules: Contacts, Companies, Deals, Activities, Comments

## Scenario
Jordan Ellis, a purchasing contact for Northstar Office Supplies, provided feedback after receiving assistance with a previous support issue.

The customer reported that the support experience was helpful but that communication could have been faster.

The feedback was documented to identify opportunities for improving future customer interactions.

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Created Customer Feedback Record

Created a new CRM deal titled:

**Customer Feedback – Jordan Ellis**

Associated the deal with:
- Contact: Jordan Ellis
- Company: Northstar Office Supplies

### 2. Documented Customer Feedback

Entered the following description in the deal's Comments field:

"Customer reports that the previous support experience was helpful but that communication could have been faster. Customer recommends providing more timely updates during the support process."

### 3. Updated Deal Stage

Reviewed the CRM pipeline and confirmed the deal was in:

**In progress**

This represented feedback that had been received and was awaiting review.

### 4. Added Internal Feedback Review Note

Added the following internal note to the deal timeline:

"Feedback reviewed. Customer expressed satisfaction with the support received but identified delayed communication as an area for improvement. Recommend providing more frequent status updates during future support interactions."

### 5. Created Feedback Follow-Up Activity

Created an activity titled:

**Review Customer Feedback**

Entered the following information in the Things to Do field:

"Review communication delays and identify ways to provide customers with more timely support updates."

Saved the activity to the deal timeline.

### 6. Documented Service Improvement Action

Added the following service improvement comment:

"Service improvement action: Future support interactions should include timely progress updates, especially when an issue requires investigation or additional processing. Customers should be informed when delays occur rather than waiting for them to request an update."

### 7. Verified Feedback Documentation

Confirmed that the following items appeared in the deal timeline:

- Internal feedback review note
- Review Customer Feedback activity
- Service improvement action

The deal remained in the In progress stage because the improvement action had been documented but not yet implemented or verified.

## Tools Used
- Bitrix24 CRM
- Contacts
- Companies
- Deals
- Activities
- Comments and deal timeline

## Findings
- CRM systems can be used to document customer satisfaction and dissatisfaction.
- Customer feedback provides insight into service quality.
- Internal notes help organize feedback reviews.
- Follow-up activities create accountability for reviewing customer concerns.
- Service improvement actions can be documented and tracked.
- Customer communication plays an important role in the overall support experience.

## Troubleshooting Notes
- The CRM deal pipeline was used to organize the feedback record.
- The In progress stage was retained because the feedback review and improvement actions had not been fully implemented.
- The Comment feature was used to document internal feedback and service improvement recommendations.
- The Activity feature was used to create a trackable feedback review task.

## Key Takeaways
- Customer feedback should be documented and reviewed.
- Positive feedback can still reveal opportunities for improvement.
- Internal documentation helps preserve customer service insights.
- Follow-up activities help ensure feedback receives attention.
- Service improvement recommendations should be tracked rather than simply recorded.
- Timely communication contributes to a better customer experience.
