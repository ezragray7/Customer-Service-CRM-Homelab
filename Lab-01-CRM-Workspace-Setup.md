
# Lab 01 — CRM Workspace Setup

## Overview

This lab establishes the CRM environment for the Customer Service & CRM Homelab.

The project uses Bitrix24 as a browser-based CRM platform to simulate customer relationship management and customer support workflows for a fictional company named Northstar Customer Solutions.

The purpose of this lab is to inspect the CRM workspace, identify available features, and prepare the environment for subsequent customer management exercises.

## Objectives

- Access and navigate the Bitrix24 CRM workspace.
- Identify available CRM modules and features.
- Inspect customer contact management capabilities.
- Identify available customer record fields.
- Establish the environment for future customer service exercises.
