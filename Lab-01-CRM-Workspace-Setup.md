# Lab 01 — CRM Workspace Setup

## Overview

This lab focused on exploring and verifying the CRM workspace in Bitrix24. The objective was to become familiar with the CRM navigation, inspect the Contacts and Companies modules, review available record fields, and identify the available custom-field configuration options.

The lab used a fictional business environment named **Northstar Customer Solutions**.

## Objectives

- Access and navigate the Bitrix24 CRM workspace.
- Locate the Contacts and Companies modules.
- Inspect standard contact information fields.
- Review available company information fields.
- Locate the custom-field creation interface.
- Identify available custom-field types.
- Verify that the CRM modules and configuration screens open successfully.

## Findings

The Bitrix24 CRM workspace provides separate modules for managing individual contacts and companies.

The Contacts module includes fields for personal and professional contact information, while the Companies module provides fields for business identity and contact details.

The CRM also provides a custom-field configuration interface with multiple field types. This functionality can support additional data collection requirements as the project progresses.

No custom fields or customer records were created during this lab.

## Troubleshooting Notes

- HubSpot was initially considered but its onboarding process required a valid company website for website analysis.
- The setup process could not be bypassed, so Bitrix24 was selected as the alternative browser-based CRM platform.
- Bitrix24 allowed the workspace setup process to continue without completing the company-size question.

## Key Takeaways

- CRM platforms organize customer and company information into separate record types.
- Contact records and company records serve different purposes within a CRM.
- Standard CRM fields provide a foundation for organizing customer information.
- Custom fields allow CRM administrators to extend standard record structures.
- Understanding CRM navigation and configuration options is an important first step before managing customer records.
