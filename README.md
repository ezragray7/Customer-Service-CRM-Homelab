
# Customer Service & CRM Homelab

## Overview

This project is a browser-based Customer Service and Customer Relationship Management (CRM) homelab designed to develop practical skills used in customer service, customer support, CRM administration, and business operations.

The project uses a fictional company, Northstar Customer Solutions, to simulate realistic workplace scenarios involving customer records, support inquiries, customer communications, complaints, escalations, and reporting.

All exercises are performed using free, browser-based tools whenever possible. No paid software or local virtual machines are required.

## Objectives

- Develop practical CRM navigation and administration skills.
- Create and maintain organized customer records.
- Practice customer inquiry documentation and case management.
- Develop professional customer communication skills.
- Practice complaint handling and escalation procedures.
- Track customer interactions and follow-up activities.
- Generate basic customer service reports.
- Build familiarity with business workflows and customer support operations.
- Develop transferable skills applicable to customer service, administrative support, and operations roles.
