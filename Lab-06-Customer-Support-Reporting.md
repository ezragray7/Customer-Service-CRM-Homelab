
# Lab 06 – Customer Support Reporting & Performance Metrics

## Objective
Explore Bitrix24 CRM analytics and reporting features to understand how customer support records, deal stages, contacts, companies, and employee activity can be monitored through reports.

## Platform
- Bitrix24 CRM
- Real-Time Analytics
- CRM Reports

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Real-Time Analytics
Inspected the available analytics sections:

- Sales Funnel with Conversions
- Sales Funnel
- Employee Performance
- Call Statistics
- Conversion Analytics
- Sales Intelligence
- Reports

Observed that some analytics were limited by available data or trial-version restrictions.

### 2. Sales Funnel Reporting
Reviewed the Sales Funnel and Sales Funnel with Conversions dashboards.

Observed:
- Sales Funnel with Conversions displayed a 0% conversion rate.
- The displayed deal amount was $0.
- The average deal lifespan displayed was 6.41 minutes.
- The standard Sales Funnel displayed deal information without the same level of conversion detail.

### 3. Employee Performance and Client Analytics
Inspected Employee Performance and the client reporting sections.

Observed:
- Employee Performance did not populate its graph with performance data.
- Regular Clients contained no data.
- Top Grossing Clients contained no data.

### 4. Call Statistics
Opened Call Statistics and reviewed the chart.

Observed:
- No call statistics were available.
- No call records had been entered for this exercise.

### 5. Sales Intelligence
Inspected:
- Advertising ROI
- Traffic Report

Observed:
- Both sections required advertising or traffic sources to be configured.
- No marketing sources were configured for this project.

### 6. Deal Reports
Inspected the available Deal Reports:

- Won Deals: No data.
- Deals by Contact: Jordan Ellis appeared.
- Deals by Company: Northstar Office Supplies appeared.
- Deals by Sales Representative: The responsible sales representative appeared.
- Expected Sales: No data.
- Overdue Deals: No data.
- Deals by Stage: Displayed all three practice deals as In progress.

### 7. Product Reports
Inspected Product Sales.

Observed:
- No product sales data was available.
- No completed product sales transactions had been created.

### 8. Lead Reports
Inspected the available Lead Reports:

- Leads by Manager: No data.
- Leads by Stage: No data.
- Leads by Source: No data.

No lead records had been created for this exercise.

### 9. Activity Reports
Inspected Managers' Activities.

Observed:
- The report displayed no data, despite activities being recorded within the practice deal timelines.
- The reason for the empty report was not confirmed.

## Security Analysis
- Reviewed customer and company reporting relationships.
- Observed how CRM records can be organized by contact, company, deal stage, and responsible employee.
- Used fictional customer information throughout the project.
- Avoided marking unresolved support cases as completed or won simply to populate reporting dashboards.

## Key Takeaways
- CRM analytics provide visibility into customer records and business activity.
- Deal-stage reports can help monitor active cases and identify where work is concentrated.
- Contact and company reports help organize customer activity by individual and organization.
- Employee reports can provide insight into responsibility and performance.
- Reporting accuracy depends on the availability and quality of CRM records.
- Empty reports do not necessarily indicate a system failure; they may reflect missing records or unmet reporting criteria.
- Some analytics features may require additional configuration or access beyond the available trial functionality.
