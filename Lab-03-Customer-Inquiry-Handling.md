
# Lab 03 – Customer Inquiry Handling

## Overview
This lab focused on using Bitrix24 CRM to document, organize, and track a customer inquiry from initial entry through customer communication and follow-up.

A fictional customer contacted Northstar Office Supplies because they had placed an order five business days earlier but had not received a shipping confirmation.

## Objectives
- Create and organize a customer inquiry.
- Associate an inquiry with an existing customer.
- Track inquiry progress using CRM deal stages.
- Create a follow-up activity with a deadline.
- Document internal investigation notes.
- Record customer progress updates.

## Environment
- CRM Platform: Bitrix24
- Application Type: Browser-based CRM
- CRM Modules: Contacts, Companies, Deals, Activities, Comments

## Scenario
Jordan Ellis, a purchasing contact for Northstar Office Supplies, reported that an order had been placed five business days earlier but no shipping confirmation had been received.

The customer requested assistance checking the order status and receiving an update.

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Created Customer Inquiry Deal
Created a CRM deal with the title:

**Order Status Inquiry – Jordan Ellis**

Associated the deal with the existing customer contact, Jordan Ellis.

### 2. Documented the Customer Inquiry
Entered the following inquiry description in the deal's Comments field:

"Customer reports placing an order five business days ago but has not received a shipping confirmation. Customer requests assistance checking the order status and receiving an update."

### 3. Updated Deal Stage
Reviewed the available CRM pipeline stages and moved the deal to:

**In progress**

This represented an active customer inquiry requiring investigation.

### 4. Created Follow-Up Activity
Created a follow-up activity titled:

**Investigate Missing Shipping Confirmation**

Saved the activity to the deal.

The CRM displayed a deadline of Thursday, November 19, 2026, at 1:00 PM.

### 5. Added Internal Investigation Note
Added an internal note to the deal timeline:

"Order status review requested. Shipping confirmation has not yet been verified. Awaiting order-status information before providing the customer with an update."

### 6. Documented Customer Progress Update
Added a customer progress update to the deal timeline:

"Customer update: We are reviewing the order status and checking why a shipping confirmation has not been received. We will provide an update once the order information has been verified."

Verified that the activity and both comments appeared in the deal timeline.

## Tools Used
- Bitrix24 CRM
- Contacts
- Companies
- Deals
- Activities
- Comments and deal timeline

## Findings
- CRM deals can be used to organize and track customer inquiries.
- Customer records can be associated with deals for easier reference.
- Deal stages provide visibility into inquiry progress.
- Follow-up activities help track outstanding tasks and deadlines.
- Internal notes preserve investigation details.
- Customer updates document communication throughout the inquiry process.

## Troubleshooting Notes
- The available deal type and source options did not include suitable selections, so those fields were left unchanged.
- The Comments field was used to document the original inquiry.
- The Comment button was used to add internal and customer progress notes to the deal timeline.

## Key Takeaways
- Customer inquiries should be documented clearly and consistently.
- CRM records help maintain organized customer histories.
- Follow-up activities support accountability.
- Internal documentation and customer communication serve different purposes.
- Customer updates should communicate progress without claiming unverified results.
