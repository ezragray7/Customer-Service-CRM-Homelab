
# Lab 09 – Customer Segmentation & Account Management

## Objective
Explore customer segmentation and business account management capabilities within Bitrix24 CRM. Learn how to classify contacts and companies, establish customer relationships, and use CRM filters to identify specific customer groups.

## Environment
- Platform: Bitrix24
- Module: CRM
- Sections: Contacts and Companies
- Existing practice records:
  - Jordan Ellis (Contact)
  - Northstar Office Supplies (Company)

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Inspected Contact Segmentation Fields

Opened the Jordan Ellis contact record and reviewed its classification fields.

Verified:
- Contact Type: Clients
- Source: Call

Identified the available contact classifications:
- Clients
- Suppliers
- Partners
- Other

These classifications help distinguish customers from other business relationships.

### 2. Inspected Company Account Classifications

Opened the Northstar Office Supplies company record and inspected its account classification fields.

Identified the available Company Type options:
- Client
- Supplier
- Partner
- Competitor
- Other

This classification system allows businesses to organize company accounts according to their relationship with the organization.

### 3. Inspected Industry Categories

Reviewed the available Industry classifications in the company record.

Available categories included:
- Information Technology
- Telecommunication
- Manufacturing
- Banking Services
- Consulting
- Finance
- Government
- Delivery
- Entertainment
- Non-profit
- Other

Industry classifications can help organizations group business accounts by market sector.

### 4. Verified Contact and Company Relationships

Opened Northstar Office Supplies and inspected its Company Contacts section.

Verified that Jordan Ellis was associated with Northstar Office Supplies.

This demonstrated how CRM systems connect individual contacts to business accounts, allowing employees to maintain customer relationship information at both levels.

### 5. Inspected Contact Filtering Capabilities

Navigated to CRM → Contacts and explored the available filtering options.

Basic filters included:
- First Name
- Last Name
- Created By
- Modified By
- Phone
- Email
- Responsible Person

Additional available fields included:
- Source
- Contact Type
- Company Name

This demonstrated how CRM filters can be expanded to support more specific customer searches.

### 6. Performed Client Contact Segmentation

Applied the Contact Type filter and selected Clients.

Verified that Jordan Ellis appeared in the filtered results.

This demonstrated how customer contacts can be separated from suppliers, partners, and other contact categories.

### 7. Performed Company-Based Contact Filtering

Applied the Company Name filter using Northstar Office Supplies.

Verified that Jordan Ellis appeared in the results.

This demonstrated how employees can identify contacts associated with a particular business account.

### 8. Inspected Company Filtering Capabilities

Navigated to CRM → Companies and reviewed the available filtering options.

Basic filters included:
- Company Name
- Created By
- Modified By
- Phone
- Email
- Responsible Person

Additional available fields included:
- Company Type
- Industry

These filtering options allow employees to organize and locate business accounts according to their classification and industry.

### 9. Performed Client Company Segmentation

Applied the Company Type filter and selected Client.

Verified that Northstar Office Supplies appeared in the filtered results.

This demonstrated how CRM users can isolate customer accounts from suppliers, partners, competitors, and other business relationships.

## Security Analysis

- Customer records should contain accurate and relevant information.
- Contact and company classifications help employees distinguish different business relationships.
- Access to customer records should be limited to authorized personnel.
- Customer contact information should be handled responsibly.
- CRM filters can help employees locate relevant records without unnecessarily exposing unrelated customer information.
- Maintaining accurate contact-to-company relationships improves account management and record consistency.

## Key Takeaways

- Bitrix24 supports contact segmentation using customer type and acquisition source.
- Company records support additional classifications, including competitors and industry categories.
- Individual contacts can be associated with company accounts.
- Contact filters can identify customers by type, source, and company.
- Company filters can identify accounts by company type and industry.
- CRM segmentation makes customer information easier to organize, search, and manage.
- Accurate customer classifications support more effective customer relationship management.

- Contact-to-company relationship management
- CRM filtering and search
- Customer data organization
- Account relationship management
