# Lab 02 — Customer Record Management

## Overview

This lab focused on creating, organizing, associating, and updating customer records using Bitrix24 CRM.

A fictional business named Northstar Customer Solutions was used as the training environment. A fictional company record and customer contact were created to practice managing business and customer information.

## Objectives

- Create a company record.
- Enter and organize company information.
- Create a customer contact.
- Associate a contact with an existing company.
- Update an existing customer record.
- Verify that changes are saved correctly.

## Environment

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| CRM Platform | Bitrix24 |
| Access Method | Web browser |
| Project | Customer Service & CRM Homelab |
| Business Scenario | Northstar Customer Solutions |
| Lab Type | Customer and company record management |

## Steps Performed

### 1. Created a Company Record

Created a fictional company record with the following information:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Company Name | Northstar Office Supplies |
| Company Type | Customer |
| Industry | Other |
| Phone | 555-010-2040 |
| Email | contact@northstar-example.com |
| Website | https://northstar-example.com |
| Address | 100 Market Street, Orlando, FL 32801 |
| Comments | Fictional customer company used for CRM training. |

The Retail industry option was not available, so Other was selected.

The Comments field was used because a dedicated Notes field was unavailable.

### 2. Created a Customer Contact

Created a fictional customer contact associated with Northstar Office Supplies.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| First Name | Jordan |
| Last Name | Ellis |
| Position | Purchasing Coordinator |
| Phone | 555-010-2041 |
| Email | jordan.ellis@example.com |
| Company | Northstar Office Supplies |
| Comments | Primary purchasing contact for Northstar Office Supplies. |

### 3. Associated the Contact with the Company

Linked Jordan Ellis to the existing Northstar Office Supplies company record.

Verified that the contact displayed the correct associated company.

### 4. Updated an Existing Customer Record

A simulated customer update was received indicating that Jordan Ellis had been promoted.

Updated the Position field:

- Previous Position: Purchasing Coordinator
- Updated Position: Senior Purchasing Coordinator

Saved the changes and verified that the updated position appeared correctly in the contact record.

## Findings

The Bitrix24 CRM allows users to maintain separate company and individual contact records while associating contacts with the appropriate company.

The contact record can be updated as customer information changes without creating a duplicate contact.

Some fields may not contain the exact options needed for a particular business scenario. In this exercise, the Other industry category and Comments field were used as alternatives.

## Troubleshooting Notes

- The Retail industry option was unavailable, so Other was selected.
- A dedicated Notes field was unavailable, so the company training description was entered in Comments.
- The contact was linked to the existing company record rather than creating a duplicate company.

## Key Takeaways

- Company records organize business-level information.
- Contact records organize individual customer information.
- Contacts can be associated with existing company records.
- CRM records should be updated when customer information changes.
- Verifying saved changes helps maintain accurate customer information.
- Alternative fields can be used when the CRM does not provide an exact field or category.
