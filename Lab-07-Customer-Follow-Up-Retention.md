
# Lab 07 – Customer Follow-Up & Retention

## Objective
Practice customer relationship management and retention techniques using Bitrix24 CRM. This lab focuses on scheduling customer follow-ups, documenting customer relationship activities, and maintaining company-level retention records.

## Platform
- Bitrix24 CRM
- Contacts
- Companies
- Activity Management

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Reviewed Existing Customer Contact
Opened the existing Jordan Ellis contact record.

Observed:
- Reviewed the customer's existing contact information.
- Inspected the contact activity timeline.
- Confirmed that the timeline initially displayed the contact creation activity.

### 2. Scheduled Customer Satisfaction Follow-Up
Created a new activity directly from Jordan Ellis's contact record.

Activity title:
- Customer Satisfaction Follow-Up

Description:
- Follow up with Jordan Ellis to check whether the previous support experience met expectations, confirm that any outstanding concerns have been addressed, and identify opportunities to improve the customer relationship.

Scheduled the activity for seven days after creation.

Verified that the activity appeared in the contact timeline with its scheduled date.

### 3. Documented Customer Relationship Information
Added a comment to Jordan Ellis's contact record:

"Customer relationship note: A satisfaction follow-up has been scheduled to evaluate the customer's previous support experience, identify any unresolved concerns, and encourage continued communication. Maintaining timely follow-ups can help strengthen customer satisfaction and retention."

### 4. Reviewed Company Contact Relationships
Opened the Northstar Office Supplies company record.

Verified:
- Jordan Ellis appeared in the company's associated contacts.
- The existing contact-to-company relationship was intact.

### 5. Documented Company Retention Information
Added a comment to the Northstar Office Supplies company record:

"Customer retention note: Northstar Office Supplies is an active customer account associated with Jordan Ellis. Maintaining regular communication, monitoring support experiences, and following up on outstanding concerns can help strengthen the business relationship and encourage long-term customer retention."

### 6. Final Verification
Returned to Jordan Ellis's contact record and verified:
- Customer Satisfaction Follow-Up remained listed.
- The scheduled date was visible.
- The customer relationship comment was saved.

## Security Analysis
- Used existing fictional customer records.
- Maintained accurate customer-to-company associations.
- Documented follow-up activities without claiming that customer communication had already occurred.
- Used CRM comments to maintain a record of customer relationship management efforts.

## Key Takeaways
- Scheduled follow-ups help organizations maintain consistent customer communication.
- Customer satisfaction checks can help identify unresolved concerns.
- Contact-level notes provide useful context for future customer interactions.
- Company-level retention notes help maintain a broader view of business relationships.
- Linking individual contacts to company records improves customer relationship visibility.
- Documenting follow-up activities supports accountability and continuity of customer service.
