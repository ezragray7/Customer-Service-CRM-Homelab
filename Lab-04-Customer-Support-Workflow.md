
# Lab 04 – Customer Support Workflow & Issue Resolution

## Overview
This lab focused on managing a customer support issue through Bitrix24 CRM. The exercise simulated a customer reporting an incorrect item in an order and requesting assistance with obtaining a replacement.

The lab demonstrated how to document customer complaints, track investigation progress, create follow-up activities, maintain internal notes, and communicate customer updates.

## Objectives
- Create and organize a customer support case.
- Associate a support case with an existing customer.
- Document a customer complaint.
- Track support case progress using CRM deal stages.
- Create an investigation activity.
- Document internal investigation notes.
- Record customer progress updates.
- Document an appropriate resolution procedure.

## Environment
- CRM Platform: Bitrix24
- Application Type: Browser-based CRM
- CRM Modules: Contacts, Companies, Deals, Activities, Comments

## Scenario
Jordan Ellis, a purchasing contact for Northstar Office Supplies, reported receiving an incorrect item in an order.

The customer requested assistance correcting the order and arranging a replacement.

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Created Customer Support Case
Created a new CRM deal titled:

**Incorrect Item Received – Jordan Ellis**

Associated the deal with the existing customer contact, Jordan Ellis.

### 2. Documented Customer Complaint
Entered the following description in the deal's Comments field:

"Customer reports receiving an incorrect item in their order. Customer requests assistance with correcting the order and arranging a replacement."

### 3. Updated Support Case Status
Reviewed the available CRM pipeline stages and changed the deal status from:

**New → In progress**

This represented an active investigation into the customer's complaint.

### 4. Created Investigation Activity
Created a follow-up activity titled:

**Investigate Wrong Item**

Entered the following details in the Things to Do field:

"Review the order, identify the incorrect item, verify the correct item, and determine the replacement process."

Saved the activity to the deal timeline.

### 5. Added Internal Investigation Note
Added the following internal investigation note:

"Investigation started. The customer's order details need to be reviewed to identify the incorrect item and verify the correct replacement before communicating a resolution."

### 6. Documented Customer Progress Update
Added the following customer progress update:

"Customer update: We have started reviewing your order to identify the incorrect item. We will provide an update once the correct replacement has been verified."

### 7. Documented Resolution Procedure
Added a resolution procedure comment:

"Resolution procedure: Verify the original order, confirm the incorrect item, identify the correct replacement, obtain any required approval, and communicate the replacement arrangements to the customer. Do not close the case until the replacement issue has been addressed."

### 8. Verified Support Case Status
Confirmed that the deal remained in the **In progress** stage.

The case was intentionally left open because the replacement had not actually been verified or completed.

## Tools Used
- Bitrix24 CRM
- Contacts
- Companies
- Deals
- Activities
- Comments and deal timeline

## Findings
- CRM deals can be used to track customer support issues.
- Associating customer records with deals improves customer history management.
- Deal stages provide visibility into support case progress.
- Follow-up activities help organize investigation tasks.
- Internal notes preserve important investigation details.
- Customer updates help maintain communication throughout the support process.
- Resolution procedures establish clear steps before closing a support case.

## Troubleshooting Notes
- The available CRM pipeline used sales-oriented stage labels.
- The In progress stage was selected to represent an active investigation.
- The Close Deal stage was intentionally not selected because the replacement had not been completed.
- The activity title was shortened to "Investigate Wrong Item" to fit the interface's character limit.

## Key Takeaways
- Customer complaints should be documented accurately.
- Support cases should remain open while investigations are ongoing.
- Internal notes and customer updates serve different purposes.
- Follow-up activities help ensure outstanding tasks are tracked.
- A case should not be closed until the customer's issue has been addressed and resolution has been verified.
