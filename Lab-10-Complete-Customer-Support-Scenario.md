
# Lab 10: Complete Customer Support Scenario

## Objective
Demonstrate an end-to-end customer support workflow using Bitrix24 CRM. This lab brings together customer records, deal management, investigation activities, customer communication, feedback review, and follow-up procedures.

## Environment
- Platform: Bitrix24 CRM
- Project: Customer-Service-CRM-Homelab
- Customer: Jordan Ellis
- Associated Company: Northstar Office Supplies

## Scenario Overview
Three existing customer support cases associated with Jordan Ellis were reviewed:

1. Incorrect Item Received
2. Order Status Inquiry
3. Customer Feedback

The objective was to examine the available case history, document investigation procedures, record customer communication, and identify service improvements.

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Incorrect Item Received
Reviewed the deal associated with an incorrect item reported by the customer.

**Existing activity:** Investigate Wrong Item

**Activity description:**
Review the order, identify the incorrect item, verify the correct item, and determine the replacement process.

**Investigation comment:**
Investigation started. The customer's order details need to be reviewed to identify the incorrect item and verify the correct replacement before communicating a resolution.

**Customer update:**
We have started reviewing your order to identify the incorrect item. We will provide an update once the correct replacement has been verified.

**Resolution procedure:**
Verify the original order, confirm the incorrect item, identify the correct replacement, obtain any required approval, and communicate the replacement arrangements to the customer. Do not close the case until the replacement issue has been addressed.

### 2. Order Status Inquiry
Reviewed the customer's order-status inquiry involving a missing shipping confirmation.

**Existing activity:** Investigate missing shipping confirmation

**Status review comment:**
Order status review requested. Shipping confirmation has not yet been verified. Awaiting order-status information before providing the customer with an update.

**Customer update:**
We are reviewing the order status and checking why a shipping confirmation has not been received. We will provide an update once the order information has been verified.

The shipping status was not independently verified during this exercise. The case therefore remains open.

### 3. Customer Feedback and Service Improvement
Reviewed customer feedback identifying delayed communication as an area for improvement.

**Feedback review:**
Feedback reviewed. Customer expressed satisfaction with the support received but identified delayed communication as an area for improvement. Recommend providing more frequent status updates during future support interactions.

**Service improvement action:**
Future support interactions should include timely progress updates, especially when an issue requires investigation or additional processing. Customers should be informed when delays occur rather than waiting for them to request an update.

**Existing activity:** Review customer feedback

**Activity description:**
Review communication delays and identify ways to provide customers with more timely support updates.

## Customer Follow-Up
The Jordan Ellis contact record also contained a scheduled Customer Satisfaction Follow-Up activity.

The activity was intended to:
- Evaluate the customer's previous support experience.
- Confirm whether outstanding concerns have been addressed.
- Identify opportunities to improve the customer relationship.

A customer relationship comment was also recorded to emphasize communication, unresolved concerns, and customer retention.

## Case Status Verification
All three deals were reviewed on the CRM Deals board.

| Support Case | Verified Stage |
|---|---|
| Incorrect Item Received | In Progress |
| Order Status Inquiry | In Progress |
| Customer Feedback | In Progress |

No case was marked resolved because the available records did not establish that the underlying issues had been fully addressed.

## Security Analysis
- Maintained customer-related investigation notes within the CRM.
- Avoided recording unverified shipping or replacement information as fact.
- Preserved open case statuses until resolution could be confirmed.
- Documented customer communication and follow-up requirements.

## Key Takeaways
- A structured investigation helps prevent premature case closure.
- Customers should receive progress updates while investigations are ongoing.
- CRM timelines provide a record of activities and customer communications.
- Customer feedback can identify opportunities to improve support procedures.
- Case status should accurately reflect the verified outcome.

- CRM case and deal management
- Customer support documentation
- Issue investigation procedures
- Customer communication
- Follow-up activity management
- Customer feedback analysis
- Service improvement recommendations
- Case status verification
